I am Jacob (Yizhou) Zhu, a graduate of New York University with a B.A. in Mathematics and Data Science. I will begin the M.S.E. program in Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania, where I plan to deepen my study of robotics and intelligent systems.

My current research interests center on Embodied AI, robot manipulation, and planning. As a Research Intern at Nanjing University, I contribute to research on embodied agent loops and robotic-arm manipulation through the AdaClearGrasp and EmbodiedClaw projects, while also exploring algorithms for dexterous-hand control.

My earlier work in data science and finance includes quantitative research at Soochow Securities, machine-learning applications in fraud detection and credit-risk modeling at China Construction Bank, and projects in political prediction, healthcare analytics, and music classification. This background continues to shape how I approach learning, perception, and decision-making in embodied systems.

I am interested in building reliable agents that connect high-level reasoning with closed-loop perception and physical action. I am especially curious about planning and learning methods that can improve manipulation across robotic arms and dexterous hands.
